# Sundays with Alan

Reaching for the recycling bin by our back glass sliding door, I looked up to see Reggie the dog smiling and wagging his tail at me. Reggie is a sweet and friendly pit mix. He is black with a white stripe on his face. He is not my dog. Atlanta is a fun place where you're likely to encounter a stray every few months or so. Reggie had a minor cut on his leg and looked like he was scrapping with some of our neighborhood dogs. He made himself at home on our back deck, and after we gave him some food and water, Reggie decided he lived here now. Unfortunately for Reggie, we are a one dog household, and our dog Brooks very much does not like other dogs. Since Reggie showed up on a Sunday, we had to keep him overnight and wait until Monday morning for animal control to show up. We've had some strays show up that we literally couldn't do anything for since the shelters are usually full. However, Reggie was technically injured so the county decided to take him in. *Tuesday update*, Reggie's owners finally found Jess's social media post.
