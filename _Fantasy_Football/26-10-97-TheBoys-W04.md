# My team sucks .. ?

We're jumping straight to the data nugget this week. Having only broken 100 points a single time in four games, I grew curious how historically bad my 2026 is going. I calculated the average scores for all 4 game stretches (regular season) from 2019-2025. Surprisingly, there were several stretches worse than the one I'm in. I have averaged 95.48 points per week. That ranks about the 8th percentile. Its not a great figure, and it gets even worse when you consider we started superflex in 2022. Since 2022, of 516 qualifying 4 game stretches there have only been 14 worse (2.7th percentile). I'd considered digging into all 14 to see how many were affected by injury vs. people not setting their lineups, but that would take too long. This was however, the second worst opening four games in league history including years without superflex and over a point per game lower than the worst superflex start in league history.
