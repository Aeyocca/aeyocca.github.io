## Matchups

Too Many Bengals (183.42) swept the floor with My Team Sucks (118.62). This one was almost over before it started on Sunday morning as Jahmyr Gibbs (44.2) ran all over the Patriots in Germany as the Lions dropped 35 points. Things were looking up for MTS in the early window Sunday. Having seen the news KW3 missed Friday's practice with a groin injury, I added Emmett Johnson (35.1) from waivers on Saturday who was able to cut into the Gibbs induced lead after housing a pair of screen passes. TMB's Jalen Coker (0.3) failed to pull in any of his four targets and Denzel Boston (2.6) was in and out of the game injured. MTS's Jordyn Tyson (19) saw 13 targets, but his Packers stack were owned at home against the Vikings, combining for just 18.5 points. The dud from that pair alone was enough to let TMB pull away with Brock Purdy's 389 passing yards and 31.66 fantasy points in the "anyone can score anytime" Cowboys game. To add insult to injury, Dem Stillers got wrecked by the Bengals and Ja'Marr Chase's 231 receiving yards (more passing yards than Jordan Love had). What made this matchup more exciting was the race to high point scorer on the week between TMB and Exciting Whites. TMB stubbornly has stuck with Omarion Hampton and it finally paid off this week as he dominated the Ravens for 105 yards and a TD on Monday night to edge out Jeff by 7 points.

Speaking of Jeff, Exciting Whites (176.84) out muscled Mr. Big Chests (154.86). To cut straight to the chase, Jeff had two players in the 86 combined point 49ers Cowboys game. Dak Prescott (35.42) fell down big early and was playing catchup all game while CMC (46.3) was a hot knife through butter against the Dallas defense. The Cowboys announced they are reassigning their defensive coordinator Monday and I wonder if thats the first DC that CMC has gotten canned. MBC probably felt good before that game with Amon-Ra St. Brown (25.8) nabbing a pair of TDs on home soil in Germany and T-Law (27.78) connecting early and often with Parker Washington (28.2). Tucker Kraft (2.9) was stuck pass blocking (unsuccessfully) against the Vikings and Chris Olave (6) drew all of the coverage away from Jordyn Tyson. With the two monster performances, Jeff just needed a few supplemental players. Bhayshul Tuten (20.3) carried the ball 26 times despite missing most of the week's practices with an injury and Drake London (16.9) racked up 100 yards before leaving in the 4th quarter with a concussion. Justin Jefferson (16.4) might finally be relevant again down the home stretch for Exciting Whites with the return of Cousins. MBC catches a tough break in this one, but still has 6 wins. Can't imagine what that's like.

Dad of Two (130.46) backed into a win against Ur Other Bye (94.02). Has anyone heard from Sweats lately? No defense, but the defense he had ended up with -6. Saquon Barkley started though he was on bye. Two tight ends started even though one was Jake Ferguson who was inactive. All that, and still more points than all but three teams this week. George Pickens (43.5) came alive against the 49ers with three TDs on 18 targets. James Cook (21.7) cooked up a solid game against the Jets and the Panthers' offense ran through Chuba Hubbard (34.2). There was literally a go ahead drive ending with less than 2 minutes in the game that was six straight plays and 68 yards to Chuba. That trio of players alone was enough to get past this week's lowest scorer in Ur Other Bye. Patrick Mahomes (35.42) dissected the Falcons defense for 5 TDs, though Rashee Rice (13.2) didn't score. Alvin Kamara carried the ball only four times, but scored twice for 16.4 fantasy points. Given the early start in Germany, Taylor probably missed the news that Rhamondre was starting and rolled out Corey Kiner for 2.5 points. Omar Cooper Jr. (0) was injured while blocking on the first play of the game, and Tyler Warren (2.4) averaged a solid 0.5 yards per reception. With Puka on IR, that probably sinks his season, welcome to the bottom!

Keith's Top-Notch Team (127.16) prevailed against Team Tendonitis (107.24). KTNT was off to a hot start with Jayden Daniels (22.76) on Thursday night. Opting to start 4 RBs payed off as all four were in the top 27 at the position. Tony Pollard (23.9) has been on a tear the last three weeks as the Titans are reported to have changed their offensive system to better match Pollard's skill set. Team Tendonitis answered on Thursday with Cam Skattebo (27.2) rushing in a pair of scored. Josh Allen (23.34) put up a decent day thanks to a pair of rushing TDs and now has two rushing TDs in 4 of 9 games this year. Allen is tied for the league lead in rushing TDs with Gibbs at 11 and Joe Burrow is #2 at QB with 3 rushing TDs. Kenneth Walker's groin injury led to Marshawn Lloyd (6.9) getting the start who honestly did a lot better than most of the players on the Packers' offense. Jameson Williams (9) didn't hit any home runs on his 7 targets and Tee Higgins (11.6) was entirely unnecessary for the Bengals to crush the Steelers on Sunday night. TT was also slowed down by Brock Bowers (3.8) injuring his ankle on the opening drive and leaving shortly after. KTNT's Harold Fannin Jr. (16.1) filled in as the TE3 on the week with his fourth consecutive double digit fantasy day in a week where the top 10 TE finishes included the likes of Ben Sinnott, Drew Sample, and Gavin Bartholomew.

Moore Brown Boutte (116.62) slid past Token Female (105.96) validating my upset pick of the week. It was tough sledding for both teams in this matchup. Token Female's Malik Nabers (7.4) was a dud on Thursday night while MBB's Jared Goff (22.42) dominated early Sunday morning against the Patriots. Token Female's Malik Washington (13.36) provided his steady dose of fantasy points while JSN (31.1) exploded in the third quarter after being held to 11 yards through 2.5 quarters. MBB's DJ Moore (19.4) narrowly scored a TD before fumbling the ball and flopping like a fish to recover it, but he had already broken the plane of the endzone. Ryan Flournoy (19.1) contributed despite being one of the lowest scoring fantasy players in the 49ers Cowboys game. Token Female's Geno Smith (5.92) was rocked by a rough sack early in the game and left twice after failing to play through the pain while Sam LaPorta (2.6) wasn't necessary for the Lions again this week. Tyler Shough (10.98) may not be a very good QB after all with his third straight game throwing only a single passing TD and going 0-3 in that stretch. That or he just has one of the worst o-line/running games but you don't see Stroud complaining. I usually think Token Female has an issue at RB, but old man Aaron Jones Sr (12.5) and sub 4 YPC Quinshon Judkins (16.1) exceeded their projections this week. Despite down games from McBride (8.7, maybe the new normal with Beck?) and Zach Charbonnet (9.4, actually not bad for a committee), Moore Brown Boutte needed a single point from Jacoby Brissett (12.8) in his Ravens debut. Good thing he didn't need a win as the Chargers claimed their first win of the year over the shit birds. Can you imagine going 10 weeks with only 1 or 2 wins? Couldn't be me...

Nasty Gworls (119.66) ghosted $81k team (102.2). Nasty Gworls shot out to a lead on Thursday night with Isaiah Likely's 27.7 TE1 performance against the Commanders against $81k team's 6.4 point Chig Okonkwo. The week seemed sunk early for $81k team as Drake Maye (3.02) broke his ankle at the end of a promising opening drive. Nico Collins (24.4) and CeeDee (20.2) tried to work their team back in the mix, but Nasty Gworls kept them at arms length with steady games from Breece Hall (14.8) and Ollie Gordon (15.5). Jonathan Taylor (12.3) was finally handed the rock more now that the trade rumors are gone, but surprisingly he has yet to rush for more than 100 yards in a game this year (5 games with 90+ though). $81k team has started the wrong Rams RB in 4 of the last 5 weeks as Blake Corum extended his rushing TD streak to 5 games in a row on Steve's bench. Kyren Williams (5.2) averaged less than 2 yards per carry against the Cardinals while Javonte Williams (7.7) didn't fare much better after the Cowboys fell down big to the 49ers. By the end of the late window, Nasty Gworls stood pat with a 2 point lead and DK Metcalf. Thankfully for Nasty Gworls, drops aren't point subtractions as DK only pulled in 2 of 6 targets in the Steelers' whooping by the Bengal. On the bright side for Steve, Kirk Cousins (16.38) has returned to post achilles injury form and because of the trade to the Vikings, he will not have a bye week this year.
