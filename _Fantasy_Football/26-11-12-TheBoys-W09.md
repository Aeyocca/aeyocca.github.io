## Matchups 

Exciting Whites (126.48) got the free win this week against My Team Sucks (117.38). On Thursday night it was the Whites' Tuten against MTS's Derrick Henry. Tuten was injured heading into the game, but did score from one yard out early. After the score, I texted Jeff that was probably going to be enough to outscore DH. Indeed it was as Henry only reached 5.58 points on 17 carries being stuck with a 3rd string QB. Tuten had a rough game himself (8.9), not being needed in the Jag's blowout victory. MTS's week didn't get any better aside from the Jordan Love - Christian Watson stack that exploded for 66.28 combined points. In fact, aside from those two, the Lions DST were the only others to score double digit points. Exciting Whites had a better Sunday. DeMario Douglas (15.2) continued what has been quietly a solid 5 week stretch playing catch-up against the Packers. Dak Prescott (19.5) and CMC (22.7) were within a point of their projections taking care of business against weak defenses while Travis Kelce (17.3) got a tush push score in the second half. Love and Watson kept things fairly close heading into Sunday night since MTS still had a pair of Bears and Baker Mayfield. Though the game hit a combined 51 points, Baker was Baker (9.72) and the Bears offense manuevered without the likes of Swift (3.1) or Loveland (9.4) leaving Jeff with the W before even waking up on Monday.

Moore Brown Boutte (81.66) limped past Keith's Top-Notch Team (70.7) in what may have been the lowest scoring matchup this league has ever seen (idk, don't feel like checking right now). MBB's Bijan Robinson (26.7) was the lone bright spot in this matchup. The only other players to beat their projection were KTNT's Seahawks DST (12) and Harold Fannin Jr. (12.2) who had zero fantasy points until overtime. MBB's Zach Charbonnet (9.6) at least looked good despite splitting carries and Joe Milton (13.16) saved his fantasy day with 60 rushing yards. KTNT boldly started Zay Flowers (5.3) with the third string QB who scored a surprising amount of points for finishing the game with 6 receiving yards. MBB's team was so beat up they went with Jahan Dotson (3.6) as a flex who hasn't had more than 3 catches in a game all year. Heading into Monday night, KTNT faced a 4 point deficit with Carson Wentz against DJ Moore. In accordance with this matchup, DJ Moore underperformed with 6.6 points meaning KTNT just needed ~11 points from Wentz. Carson decided he didn't want to play QB, however, and proceeded to go 8-25 for 81 yards, two interceptions, and a lost fumble amounting to -0.76 fantasy points. Wentz was not the lowest scoring QB of the week. Justin Fields fumbled a tush push in his only play of the game. Much like that play, this week is one to forget. 

Ur Other Bye (172.92) layed the hammer against Team Tendonitis (129.16). Puka Nacua (35) continued to be the entire Ram's offense with 19 targets against the Commanders and Matthew Golden (32.4) caught all ten of his targets with 164 yards and a TD. I called my shot in the predictions that the Jaguars DST would score 20 points against Fagnano. They ended up with 13 which isn't bad, but Team Tendonitis's NYG scored 15 against Joe Milton and the Eagles. TT also go another solid game out of Tee Higgins (20.2), who leads the Bengals in receiving yards, and Brock Bowers (22.3) who may be taking a slight hit with the departure of Cousins but should still be the TE1. TT was forced into starting JK Dobbins (3.2) though he has been relegated to backup status recently. Demarcus Robinson (5.2) also go the nod over Drew Lock, but was not the highlight of the 49ers offense. Ur Other Bye's Bryce Young (21.34) came out of a short slump while Bucky Irving (28.5) continued to dominate and has been the RB1 since week 5. TT matched Bucky's output with KW3's (36.3) trio of TDs against the Jets, but down games from Josh Allen (13.86) and Jameson Williams (5) left this one far from competitive.

Nasty Gworls (109.24) snuck past Dad of Two (81.12) who took Week 9 off. Dad of Two decided not play a defense this week since his usual Steelers DST were on the bye. To be fair to him, why drop a valuable bench player like Terry McLaurin for a second defense? Sweats also missed a late inactive of George Pickens, though that wasn't official until Sunday morning. DoT started two TEs this week while leaving George Kittle on the bench, presumably since he hasn't checked his lineup since last Tuesday and Kittle was on the bye last week. Has anyone heard from Jeremy? I hope he's doing alright, and he still has twice as many wins as I do so I can't complain. Nasty Gworls didn't have the greatest week but did enough to get by. Jonathan Taylor (9.4) has been ice cold as the Colts have debated trading JT. Now that the deadline has passed, he should return to his normal 20+ carries per game. Breece Hall (11.7) returned to action for the first time since week 3 and immediately saw 19 opportunities. Jaylen Waddle (3.2) left the game injured on Sunday as is tradition while Ollie Gordon II (7.4) failed to score for only the second time since taking over for Achane. Heading into Sunday night, Nasty Gworls just needed a handful of points from the Williams/Odunze stack. The pair connected on a TD on the Bears' opening drive wrapping up this matchup early and leaving an insurmountable for DoT's James Cook to overcome.

Too Many Bengals (145.22) prevailed in the Meyer Matchup against Token Female (100.9). TMB shot out of the gates in Madrid on Sunday morning as Ja'Marr Chase (31.5) racked up 140+ receiving yards by halfway through the second quarter. That was a necessary cushion as both Josh Jacobs (0.5) and Ladd McConkey (0) were injured. Apparently Ladd was injured in pregame warmups and ended up not playing a single snap while Jacobs fractured a finger and will likely miss a few week. Token Female got a good game out of Audric Estime (14.7) who signed with the Saints earlier in the week and took an Uber to the Stadium on Sunday before carrying the ball 15 times for 72 yards and a TD. Geno Smith (7.4) struggled against the Chiefs and Tyler Shough (19.7) needed overtime for a decent score line. Michael Wilson (12) had a decent game, but literally broke his neck on the 4th play of the game and played through it in what will be his last game of 2026. JSN (12.1) also had their worst game of the year so far, though if there was a week to have it, it might as well be the one where you lose by >40 points. TMB's Brock Purdy (26.28) ran up the score against the Raiders while Luther Burder (20.7) continued his first half dominance before leaving with a concussion. Despite over 50 combined points from the QB position, TMB left the QB2 on the week CJ Stroud (36.36) on the bench. Stroud also ran up the score against the Chargers passing for 384 yards and 5 TDs. Token Female couldn't keep pace this week, but at least managed to break the 100 mark; something several teams struggled to do this week despite the only bye weeks being the Steelers and Titans.

$81k Team (154.2) stomped Mr. Big Chests (105.1). On Thursday night, MBC was looking for T-Law and Parker Washington to play bully ball against the Ravens who were playing without a QB. The Jags were able to collect 33 points, but several scores came with FGs and rushing TDs. Lawrence and Washington were set for a rough fantasy night until the Ravens ran a zero blitz late in the first half allowing Washington to score from 50 yards out. Still, the pair ended the game with under 30 combined points. $81k Team quickly closed that gap with CeeDee Lamb (33.5) who had almost 200 receiving yards in Pickens' absence. Steve finally moved off of Mark Andrews at TE for Chig Okonkwo (11.7). Despite CJ Stroud's big passing day, Nico (15.5) only managed 55 receiving yards but pulled in one of the catches of the year one-handing it off the DB's chest for a TD. MBC had some tough luck as Malik Willis (4.82) left injured and his headed for IR, though Willis was playing terribly anyway before he left. Tucker Kraft (1.9) wasn't needed in the Packers' rout of the Patriots. That rout left $81k Team's Drake Maye (21.26) in a pass-first game script, but finished with 3 interceptions. MBC did get another solid game from $6 waiver pickup Jonah Coleman (18.8) who after the trade deadline looks like the unquestioned RB1 in Denver (at least until Harvey comes off IR). Without a big performance to match Lamb, however, MBC never got close in this matchup.
