## Matchups

Dad of Two (121.5) got the free win this week against My Team Sucks (109.64). My Team Sucks looked like an actual fantasy football team on Thursday night to start the week as D'Andre Swift (19) and Colston Loveland (19.9) each scored. Early on Sunday though things evened out with a middling game from Aaron Rodgers (13.08) and a dud from Germie Bernard (5.7). In the early window, Dad of Two also struggled with the Tet McMillan/Chuba combo combining for only 18 points in the Buccaneer's rout of the Panthers. With a pair of players on bye, Dad of Two started a second TE Michael Mayer (6.2) in the flex and KC Concepcion (23.2) in the superflex spot. Concepcion's big game would have been good for a QB7 finish on the week and was more than twice as good as his best game as a pro so far. Derrick Henry (5.9) had a second straight dud game after MTS traded for him as Lamar missed another week, and the Love-Watson duo combined for only 28.66 points in a disappointingly low scoring Packers Lions game. Heading into Monday night, MTS had a 19 point lead against both Saquon and Pickens. Saquon was nursing a hamstring injury but was active. On the Eagles opening drive it was all Saquon. After a 29 yard carry, he came gingerly off the field and Troy Aikmen suggested Saquon wouldn't return to the game. Well two plays later, Barkley bounced a run outside and scored on a play where he hit his highest top speed of the year. Thanks for the wishful thinking Troy. To make matters certain, in the second quarter George Pickens pulled in an incredible finger tip + toe tap TD to seal the victory for Dad of Two. GG everyone, see you next year.

Mr. Big Chests (125.76) took advantage of Team Tendonitis's lack of QBs (93.4). With Josh Allen on bye being his only starting QB rostered, our greedy asses in this league had rostered every other starting QB. The rest of the team tried to pick up the slack. Tee Higgins (26.8) dominated the Ravens in the second half and Jameson WIlliams (26.1) had a pair of TDs for the second consecutive week. The first dud of the year for Ken Walker (9.7) came at a good time though, as there wasn't much hope without a starting QB. MBC's week was the fourth highest scoring. Rhamondre (22.5) got things going on Thursday night at the end of the first half by housing a check down as time expired. Stevenson looked to lateral the ball after about 30 yards then looked up and couldn't believe there were no defenders in his way. On Sunday, Amon-Ra (24.2) put together a great game considering he didn't score a TD and Deshaun Watson (19.22) ran in a score against the Titans. Chase Brown (9.8) had an average game and his lack of production the past few weeks has mudied his future outlook. MBC survives the bye weeks of ARSB, Chase Brown, T-Law, and Parker Washington 2-0.

Moore Brown Boutte (113.4) soured Nasty Gworls' (101.72) week. Most players in the matchup struggled. Nasty Gworls' DK Metcalf (7.6) caught only two of his seven targets while Jaylen Waddle (8.8) exited early with a chest injury. Isaiah Likely (3.2) couldn't keep up his super star pace of the last three weeks and Kyler Murray (13.9) looked like Kyler Murray passing for only 150 yards. MBB had their own duds in Garrett Wilson (4.6) who was missed on a pair of wide open TDs as Geno Smith's eyes went elsewhere instigating a shouting match on the sidelines. Both defenses in this matchup combined for a single point. Nasty Gworls' free agent addition Ollie Gordon (17.3) has been well worth the $40 faab as he has finished RB13 or better every week since taking over for Achane. However, MBB's superstars hit harder. Trey McBride led the way with a 27.1 point game on 13 targets while Emanuel Wilson (18.5) faced a positive game script against the Chiefs allowing him to rush 24 times for 100 yards. Heading into Monday night, Nasty Gworls had Dallas Goedert and a ~19 point deficit against Darius Cooper. Cooper (2.7) had a drop early and didn't catch a TD pass, but Goedert still needed a massive game and never got close. At least he scored enough to get the Gworls past 100 points.

Too Many Bengals (134.3) rolled against Patty Potbellies (117.42). TMB started hot in the first half Thursday night with 130 receiving yards from Luther Burden (23.7). Burden's zero catches in the second half started a cold streak from TMB. Jalen Coker (1.9) and the Panthers' offense was deleted while Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase started slow against the Ravens falling down 13-0 in the first half. The Bengals stormed back with 35 unanswered points and the Burrow-Chase duo finished with a combined 46.08 points. Jahmyr Gibbs (17.3) and Brock Purdy (14.02) fell well short of their projections, but the Potbellies could't keep pace. As the Panthers were getting smacked, PP's Bucky Irving (24) ran wild. Omar Cooper Jr. (22.5) repeated his breakout game last week and Puka Nacua (21.8) put up a solid game despite a calf injury sustained late in the week. PP's QB's combined for 14.42 points (Mahomes + Young) while Matthew Golden (9.9) didn't connect on any of his deep shots leading to a bottom half performance by the Potbellies and a 6th loss. Welcome to the bottom. You're welcome for your only win.

Keith's Top-Notch Team (150.64) decimated Exciting Whites (107.16). KTNT was without their top two picks in Lamar and Jayden Daniels this week and filled the void with Tyler Huntley (16.12) and Daniel Jones (4.62). Jordan Addison (4.3) also came up short in a plus matchup against the Colts, but the is where KTNT's woes ended. Harold Fannin (13.1) came out of his rut after averaging less than 2 points per game the prior two weeks and Zay Flowers (27.7) boosted his day with a 48 yard TD on a screen on the opening drive. Ashton Jeanty (21.6) is a check down machine despite averaging less than 4 yards per carry in all but one game this year. His 83 reception pace would have put him second to only CMC last year. The story of KTNT's squad this week was Tony Pollard (36.5) Pollard was ripping runs against the Browns all day, finishing with 129 rushing yards and three TDs. Exciting Whites couldn't keep pace. Rico Dowdle (14.6) finally looks healthy and pulled in 6 receptions while CMC (22) matched that mark while scoring on the ground. Justin Jefferson (8.4) hasn't been needed much in the Vikings offense as they improved to 6-0 while Xavier Worthy (4.1) sunk with the rest of the Chief's offense. Dak Prescott (23.28) and Drake London (19.6) put up serviceable performances, but the Whites never stood a chance.

$81k Team (150.64) came out ahead of Token Female (129.1) in the highest scoring matchup of the week. Token Female's Tyler Shough (14.82) put up plenty of yards in Paris (278) but struggled in the red zone with and interception to Payton Wilson while targetting Chris Olave. Quinshon Judkins (23.4) was efficient rushing the ball at 5.6 YPC while JSN (31.3) and Malik Nabers (18.1) continued their solid seasons. Geno Smith (24.48) even contributed in a loss to Miami. $81k team started strong on Thursday with Drake Maye (28.44) including his 50 yard check down TD to Stevenson. Nico Collins (2.6) and Dalton Schults (9.5) were a pair of duds as the Texans struggled in their overtime loss to the Giants. Kirk Cousins (20.4) looks like his pre-achilles self with the Raiders despite their recent losing streak and Kyren (18.1) accumulated a fair amount of points despite being benched for the opening drive after complaining to the media about playing time. Heading into Monday night, $81k team was down 44 points, but had the Cowboys duo and Makai Lemon. Lemon was targetted early and often while CeeDee brought in a first quarter TD. In the second half, each of Steve's players scored allowing him to cruise past Token Female. Its a shame Steve didn't play Keith this week so we could have had a tie from the two highest scorers on the week.
