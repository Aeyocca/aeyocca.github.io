# Who watches football anymore?

This year, my football viewing experience is a bit different. Last year, Riley was a potato that I could lay on the bed while I watched football all Sunday. This year, he actually has head control, and Jess actually wants to leave the house on Sundays to do stuff with him. Weird, right?? Even for primetime games, I'm often on the treadmill. That means a lot of putting in one headphone and listening to Red Zone rather than watching if I can. I've also been saving some football podcast episodes for my long runs on the weekends which means fewer "fun facts" from those I can share in these articles. I hope that hasn't been too noticeable, but I've definitely written some of these matchups from the box scores alone. Intermittently, I'll share something I actually did watch on Sunday. This Sunday, my brother came to town and we went to the Georgia Aquarium together. It was incredibly busy but well worth it as always. My favorite part is usually the Whale Shark tank that holds 6.3 million gallons of water. For reference, the largest tank at the Pittsburgh aquarium is about 100,000 gallons. This time, though, I think I liked the new touch pools the best. The Georgia Aquarium is a must if you ever stop through. Its properly rated.

## Matchups

Team Tendonitis (127.4) out kicked My Team Sucks (123.96). TT was off to a decent start with JK Dobbins (13.6) finishing the week as RB16 in a tough matchup against Seattle. On Sunday morning, MTS and the Bears built a lead thanks in part to a pair of TDs from D'Andre Swift (23). Derrick Henry (5) couldn't get anything going against the Browns and even fumbled late in their loss against the division-leaders. TT matched that dud with Cam Skattebo (0) who full goosed. Earlier in the week, Harbaugh mentioned he would be punished for his recent fumbling issue. That apparently meant being given only six snaps and zero carries on Sunday. TT was picked up by the emergence of Carnell Tate (19) and a bomb TD from Romeo Doubs (11.2) on a play that sent him straight to injured reserve. MTS kept pace with the Steelers stack (thats how you end up 1-5) of Aaron Rodgers (17.02) and Germie Bernard (25.7). Down 0-16 in the first half, this duo was juiced with a negative game script and an overtime TD. Tyler Higbee (4.2) mostly disappeared with the Rams offense against a supposedly great matchup against the Cardinals while TT's Brock Bowers (11) wasn't targetted in the first half against the Bills. Josh Allen even had one of his worse game with only 18.3 fantasy points in their 33-30 victory over the Raiders. Heading into Sunday night, MTS was hoping for some juice from the Jordan Love-Christian Watson stack. Love continued to air it out and racked up 276 passing yards, but his lone TD went to Matthew Golden and left the stack with just over combined points and a 12 point lead for MTS against just Stefon Diggs. In PPR, 12 points can accumulate quickly, but I thought Diggs would probably need a TD to eclipse 12. San Fransisco's offense hummed early and often leading to some dominant ball control and few opportunities for the Commanders' offense. Diggs even got banged up early with a helmet hitting his left hand. However, Washington's #1 receiver, Terry McLaurin, tore his ACL early and made Diggs the primary. Late in the third, Jeremy McNichols came up a yard short on 1st and goal. Between that play and the next I saw Diggs break the huddle to the left of the formation stacked behind Chig Okonkwo. I knew the pick play was coming. Sadly, the 49ers DBs couldn't beat it and let up an easy completion to Diggs which put TT ahead for good. On the bright side, Colston Loveland (18.4) might finally be worth something with the return of Caleb Williams? I'll need more than a top 5 TE to turn my season around.

Token Female (170.44) continued to rain fantasy points against Dad of Two (132.04). Dad of Two had a great week, scoring the second most points. Justin Herbert (9.76) continued to look like Kenny Pickett and Jeremy continues to stubbornly start Wan'Dale Robinson (5.2), but the rest of the squad had a good Sunday. James Cook (24.3) and Chubba Hubbard (19.1) combined for 50 rush attempts and over 260 scrimmage yards. Matt Stafford (16.88) despite throwing the game losing interception against the Cardinals managed a pair of passing TDs and the Steelers DST (11) eventually took control against the rookie Jaylon Daniels. Unfortunately for Dad of Two, he ran into the Buzz Saw this week. On Thursday night, in a very low scoring affair, Token Female's two players somehow managed to combine for 40 fantasy points (JSN / RJ Harvey). On Sunday, DeVonta Smith (20.5) was hyper targetted in Jalen Hurts' abscence and Hunter Henry (14.8) pulled in a 4th quarter TD from Drake Maye. Malik Nabers (28.5) was having an average day against the Saints until they decided to pull their starters up 34-10 with 12 minutes left in the game. The Giants switched from Winston to McCarthy who promptly threw an interception. When the Giants regained possession with 9:30 left, McCarthy started chucking it and accumulated 248 4th quarter passing yards. 95 of which went to Nabers in an almost comeback where the G-Men had 4th and 1 at the 14 down only 7 points with 12 seconds left. On the other side of the game, Token Female also had Tyler Shough (26.96) who only threw for 144 yards but had 4 total TDs. This probably won't be the last time this year the second highest scorer loses to the highest scorer on the week, but it feels really bad if the second highest scorer drops to 2-4 as a result.

Mr. Big Chests (95.7) snuck past Moore Brown Bouttee (78.52) in this week's toilet bowl (which I wasn't a part of again!!). As these teams' acronyms both begin with MB, we're going to call them C and B today. It was a lot of single digits for both squads. For B, Emanuel Wilson (5.2) came back down to Earth after a pair of solid games while Bijan (7.3) had what should be his worst game of the year. AJ Brown (5.7) appeared to have come off IR a game too early and was credited with 4 drops on Sunday. For reference, that is the same number of drops that Puka and Ja'Marr Chase each had last year.. on 351 combined targets. C's Tyler Allgeier (5.8) played second fiddle to Bam Knight as we all expected while Rhamondre (3.6) was not required against the Jets and saw zero targets after catching 3 or more passes in all but one game so far this year. C was buoyed by this week's QB1 in Trevor Lawrence (28.34) against the Texans. C's Parker Washington (10.4) was outscored by B's Jakobi Meyers (13.3), but B's QBs combined only scored ~75% of 1 Trevor Lawrence. C survives the ARSB/Chase Brown bye week and has to play without his Jaguars next week.

Nasty Gworls (124.4) took advantage of Too Many Bengals' (99.74) bye-pocalypse. TMB had Burrow, Chase, and Gibbs on bye this week. This led to the tragic decision of starting both Chargers RBs Hampton and Mitchell for a combined 8.3 points off 45 scrimmage yards. Hampton's betting line on rush yards O/U was just 11.5, he ended with 18, and is somehow still the RB20 on the year. It's been rough for RBs this year apparently.  Nasty Gworls matched the dud performance from Mitchell with two total rushing yards from Najee on four carries. Najee played so bad the Giants cut him after the game. Jaylen Waddle (6.4) has also been a victim of a poor Broncos offense. The return of Caleb Williams, however, boosted Nasty Gworls' lineup while DK Metcalf (20.3) actually decided to catch the ball this week. Isaiah Likely (23.9) has emerged as a top tier TE starting the week after I messaged Mike, "I don't really value Likely at all though". Michael Penix Jr. (23.1) started slow against the Bears as the Falcons fell down 31-7. As I said earlier, I was downtown during the game and watched as sad fans flooded the streets while Jess kept saying, "the Falcons scored again". Luckily for the Bears, the Falcons faked the comeback and fumbled the game away with 3 minutes left.

Exciting Whites (114.8) completed the comeback against Patty Potbellies (102.52). The Whites started slow on Thursday night with Bo Nix (4.48) and the Broncos historically slow offense. With 5 letters, Bo Nix has the shortest name for a starting QB in NFL history. That is still a higher value than his 4.5 yards per attempt this year (these are the kinds of podcast fun facts I mentioned in the intro). Early Sunday in London though, the Whites bounced back with Bhayshul Tuten's (24.7) pair of receiving TDs. PP's Bucky Irving (6.9) regressed from his 30 bomb last week, but Alvin Kamara (14.9) and Dameon Pierce (15) stepped up. PP did not catch Tyler Warren's late week groin injury that held him out on Sunday, but his likely replacement on the bench in Darren Waller goosed also. On Sunday night, PP's Matthew Golden (22.4) outscored Exciting Whites' Dak (13.02) setting them up ahead 18 points against CMC. The 49ers had been managing McCaffrey this season and he had only broken this threshold twice before. This week, however, their opening drive on Monday night football fed three passes to CMC. A pair of rushing TDs later left CMC as the only player this week that scored more than 30 fantasy points.

Keith's Top-Notch Team (108.36) decimated $81k Team (108.22). Each team in this matchup had a single player break 20 points. $81k Team's Drake Maye (23.8) is keeping up his fantasy success despite four drops from AJ Brown in his return. $81k Team also stubbornly sticks with Mark Andrews (1.4) who has only had one decent week in the last four. Kirk Cousins (19.52) kept balling out and is the QB6 on the year. Despite the fantasy success, three consecutive losses gives you the feeling that Mendoza's time is coming. $81k team is heavily reliant on the Cowboys and Rams offenses, both of which under performed this week. Only one of their four stacked players found the endzone (Javonte; 13) and Davante Adams (6.9) suffered a serious hamstring injury late that will knock him out 6-10 weeks. KTNT had a decent game from Daniel Jones (15.76) and the best Egbuka game (17.8) in quite some time. Ashton Jeanty (15.8) continues to produce for fantasy even while dealing with a foot injury. Zay Flowers (12.7), however, struggled in his last game without Lamar and Jaylen Warren (9.8) only managed 50 rushing yards. $81k team's Cowboys only combined for 29 points on Sunday night which left KTNT down ~21 with just Jayden Daniels left. The 49ers played ball control early leaving the first half with nearly 20 minutes of possession time and a 20-3 lead. A pair of TD drives in the second half broke open the fantasy scoring for Daniels and kept the Commanders within 1 score. Their final drive started near mid field with KTNT down almost a full point. One scramble could swing this matchup. A check down and spike left the Commanders with a 3rd and 1. On that play Daniels found Antonio Williams for 17 yards, 4 more than were needed for KTNT to pull head. That ended up being Jayden's last completed pass of the game and sealed the victory.
