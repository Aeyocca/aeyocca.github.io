# Playoffs??

Lets check in on the standings, shall we? Its something I've been not too keen to do lately...

Our leading teams, Token Female and MBC are at 6-2 and are in the same division so are fighting for the same bye week spot. They only play each other once this year with MBC holding the tie breaker (or whatever the hell Jeff has it set to I don't feel like looking it up now). Their schedules seem pretty balanced down the stretch, but we have many weeks remaining. There may not be as many weeks remaining for the basement dwellers of Ur Other Bye and My team sucks. ESPN sits the pair with a less than 10% chance at the playoffs. With low points for, they both likely need to run the table to guarantee playoffs. There is a fair amount of parity in the middle 5-3 to 3-5 teams and our longest winning/losing streaks are only at 2 games. I'm guessing 7-7 gets playoffs while 6-8 is probably the end of the road.

## Matchups

My team Sucks (136.32) doubled his wins this week against $81k team (103.14). $81k team couldn't get much going this week. Kirk Cousins (28.18) continued his magical season against the Jets and just this week lost the passing TD lead to Joe Burrow (more on him later). However, Cousins did so without the help of Tre Tucker (0) who Steve finally decided to start after a pair of 20+ point games on his bench. Steve also stubbornly starts Mark Andrews (4.4) wherever he can. To be fair, Andrews may have had a good game if the Ravens didn't quickly end up with a third string quarterback in what was supposed to be a high scoring game. Drake Maye (22.46) picked up his pace of play in the second half after falling down by 10 points to the Dolphins, but he couldn't lift $81k team much past 100 points. My team sucks had a rollercoaster of a week. On Thursday night, Jordan Love (22.52) and Christian Watson (17.3) were lighting up the Panthers early. On Watson's 6th catch he came down hard and started coughing up blood on the sidelines leading to a quick trip to the hospital. I call it a quick trip, because later in the game, Watson returned to Lambeau and some idiot on the Packers' sideline handed him back his helmet. Watson was targetted three more times in a game the Packers controlled throughout. On Sunday, Derrick Henry (14) scored a rushing TD early, but like Andrews was hamstrung by a lack of Ravens offense early in the game. Braelon Allen (23.3) had his fourth consecutive start with Breece Hall still nursing his quad injury, but Baker Mayfield (1.4) played in a monsoon that I didn't find out about until past 1pm. Heading into Monday night, My team sucks needed just six points from Loveland and Swift combined. The Bears opened the game with 8 straight plays to either Swift or Loveland on route to a 17 play, 8:32 TD drive that got the job done and doubled MTS's win column. Get rekt Steve.

Dad of Two (111.58) continued the upset train against Mr. Big Chests (104.24). DoT started hot on Thursday with the Panthers channeling their entire offense through Chuba Hubbard (24.9). Hubbard carried the ball or was targetted on their first six plays and finished the game with 29 touches. Tetairoa McMillan (11.8) wasn't as busy but put up an acceptable score line. Both squads in this matchup opted for a TE in the flex. DoT's Jake Ferguson goosed for the second straight week, but this week was due to an ankle injury. MBC also missed Sunday's weather report and started Kyle Pitts (2.6) who played in Atlantis. MBC's Trevor Lawrence (19.4) diced up the Colts defense, but did so without the help of Parker Washington (3.6). Amon-Ra (12.6) likewise had a down game and has only cracked 20 points once since week 2, though the schedule is opening up nicely for the Sun God moving forward. DoT finally wised up and left Herbert (9.78) on the bench who threw a league leading 10th interception on Sunday. Jer was carried to victory on the back of the Steelers DST (18), or rather by Deshaun Watson on his back, as Watson was sacked eight times by the Steel Curtain. MBC will regret not checking the weather as I hadn't since he left recently acquired Jonah Coleman (20.4) on the bench in favor of Pitts leading to MBC's second loss on the year.

Team Tendonitis (136.76) stomped on Nasty Gworls (79.38). The Gworls put up one of the worst weeks we've seen this year. That's saying something given the year I've been having. Shockingly, Kyler Murray (20.9) was his highest scoring player and Jaylen Waddle (14.9) was actually serviceable this week. Jonathan Taylor (6.7) sorely missed Quenton Nelson while Ollie Gordon (6.9) looked solid, but failed to score for the first time since week 2. DK Metcalf (1.4) looked decidedly not solid, dropping a wide open deep shot by juggling the ball into Denzel Ward's hands. DK is on pace for over 130 targets and less than 900 receiving yards. The Steelers are rumored to be in the trade market for a WR. I only hope DK is part of the swap, but that probably involves us sending extra picks to balance out him being part of the deal. Team Tendonitis was carried by a trio of monster games. Tee Higgins (37.5) and the Bengals lit up the Titans all day while Josh Allen (27.56) and Brock Bowers (28.2) put up their routinely dominant performances. TT survived some duds from Kenneth Walker (12.4), JK Dobbins (1.3), and Tez Johnson (0). To be fair to Tez, he was the Buccaneers' leading receiver as they failed to complete a pass 10,000 leagues under the sea.

Ur Other Bye (135.5) waved farewell to Moore Brown Bouttee (101.2). MBB struggled this week headlined by Jacoby Brissett's (4.46) benching against the Cowboys before he could rack up his usual 4th quarter garbage points. DJ Moore (16.6) scored against the Ravens while Ryan Flournoy (13.1) put up a solid game for an emergency flex start. Bijan Robinson (7.6), however, didn't follow the Buc's plan of "run the ball on every single play" in Bikini Bottom and Jared Goff (10.04) didn't need to light up the scoreboard against the Vikings. UOB had some dominant games. The Jaguars DST (21) feasted against the Quenton Nelson-less Colts and should do so again against the Ravens' third string QB on Thursday. Matthew Golden (20.9; WR7) kept up his strong season while Puka Nacua (25) accumulated enough points despite Matt Stafford's rapidly decreasing yards per pass attempt. UOB survived a trio of duds on Omar Cooper Jr (0), Tyler Warren (5.8), and Kimani Vidal (5). Okay I'm not sure that last one is a dud given our expectations of the Chargers offense, but five points still isn't that many and makes UOB's 135 score more impressive. Our week of mostly lower seeded teams winning continued in this one.

Lamar (0) injured his thumb on the first play of the game and is likely out for the year really hurting KTNT's chances, though they still have Jayden Daniels and some decent depth. With Lamar out, Zay Flowers was held without a catch. A combined zero points from your stack against your opponent's *checks notes* ... 73.58 pretty much wraps the week up on that alone. Joe Burrow and the Bengals started slow again this week with three interceptions in the first half. The second half followed last week's script as the passing offense took off for five second half passing TDs. The Titans miraculously were able to keep pace and force overtime which just led to a rushing and 6th total TD for Burrow. Chase (31.1) collected his due points and ended this contest before it got going. Credit to TMB for continuing to roster a pair of Chargers players (Hampton/Ladd) and being wise enough to start neither, though with Love going to IR and further allegations surfacing against Jacobs, he may be forced to throw them out there eventually.

Token Female (125) devastated Exciting Whites (124.68). Exciting Whites started hot on Sunday with Bhayshul Tuten (29.9) lighting up the Colts in a positive game script from start to finish. Rico Dowdle (11.6) was invovled against the Browns with 15 rushing attempts and a TD while the Rams DST (12) handled business against the Chargers. Token Female's Geno Smith (19) put up a good showing in a back and forth game against the Raiders, but had Hunter Henry (6) leave early with a foot injury while Quinshon Judkins (6.9) was stopped along with the rest of the Browns offensive players not named "Harold". Waiver wire pickup Malik Washington (15) was picked up for free this week and put up his best week of the year so far. Exciting Whites had three pieces in the KC-DEN game that exceeded the over/under, but neither Xavier Worthy (4.3) nor Kelce (11.7) scored. The Whites were also hamstrung by 1.8 points from Drake London who unfortunately was forced to play in the Mariana Trench this week. Token Female in the late window exploded with 26.8 points from Michael Wilson despite his broken ribs in the Cardinals comeback victory against the Cowboys. On Sunday night, The Commanders defense were stout against the Jalen Hurts-less Eagles and collected 15 points, leaving Token Female ~23 points short with only JSN remaining. The WR1 on the year has failed to hit that mark twice in the prior 7 weeks and it looked as if a third was on the way. Monday night football was a low scoring affair early with the Seahawks sitting at 3 points near the end of the first half. On their last drive of the first half, JSN put a pair of defenders in the blender, collecting 55 receiving yards and a TD as the Bears played zero blitz with 30 seconds left in the half. The Seahawks offense continued to struggle in the second half and the Bears went up by 17 points. With about 4 minutes remaining, Darnold connected with JSN twice, once on a double catch that almost hit the ground, and the second and final catch of the night into triple coverage. Every ounce of that was needed by Token Female who ended the week up 0.32 points.
